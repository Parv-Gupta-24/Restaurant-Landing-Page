A kitchen rooted in spice and tradition

Tandoor & Thali opened in 2018 in a restored heritage haveli. At its heart stands a clay tandoor fired with charcoal, alongside copper handis where gravies simmer for hours. Every spice blend is ground in-house daily, capturing the true essence of traditional Indian kitchens.

The menu celebrates regional vegetarian masterpieces. What doesn't change is the warmth of the room: brass accents, gentle lighting, and the timeless tradition of sharing a hearty meal together.
